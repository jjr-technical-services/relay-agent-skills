# Safe-messaging checklist

Use every row for high-sensitivity content (suicide, self-harm, a death by suicide, an
active crisis). Use the rows marked "all" for general mental-health content. The default
severity is the usual label; raise it when context makes the risk worse (youth audience,
a recent local death, paid reach).

| # | Check | Applies to | Default severity | What good looks like |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | No description of method or means, even vague | High | Must fix | Say the person died by suicide, nothing about how |
| 2 | No location of a death or attempt, no images of it | High | Must fix | No place names, landmarks, or scene photos tied to the death |
| 3 | No single-cause explanation (exam, breakup, bullying, job loss) | High | Must fix | Note that suicide is complex and usually involves many factors; mention that help works |
| 4 | Suicide not framed as a solution, an escape, or inevitable | High | Must fix | Frame as preventable in many cases, with support available |
| 5 | No crime language | All | Must fix | "Died by suicide", "death by suicide", "took their own life" only if the organization's guide allows it |
| 6 | No sensational or romanticized language, no "epidemic" framing | High | Should fix, Must fix in headlines | Plain, factual, calm wording |
| 7 | Memorial content does not glorify or present the death as a way to be remembered | High | Should fix | Celebrate the person's life, not the manner of death |
| 8 | Hope and help-seeking present | High, and all where distress is discussed | Should fix, Must fix if absent from a high-sensitivity piece | A concrete action: talk to someone, the crisis slot, how to support a friend |
| 9 | Crisis resource is the organization's approved copy, placed correctly | High | Must fix if invented or unverified | `ORGANIZATION-APPROVED CRISIS RESOURCE (insert approved wording)` slot filled verbatim |
| 10 | Statistics have context and a source and are not used to shock | All | Should fix | Pair any figure with what people can do; name the source |
| 11 | No clinical claims about content, programs, or donations | All | Must fix | Describe what was measured: participation, knowledge, self-reported confidence |
| 12 | No guilt, fear, or countdown urgency in any ask connected to a death or crisis | All | Must fix | Invitation to support the work, with no implied responsibility for a death |
| 13 | People-first, non-stigmatizing language | All | Should fix | See language-guide.md |
| 14 | Content note present when detail or personal story is included | High | Should fix | Short note at top plus crisis slot |
| 15 | Headline and preview text safe on their own | All | Must fix if method, cause, or sensational verb | Headline states the topic calmly or leads with help |
| 16 | Images: no distress imagery, no scene, no method cues | All | Must fix for scene or method, Should fix for distress | People connecting, supportive or neutral imagery |
| 17 | Lived-experience voices are recovery-oriented and consented | High | Should fix | Focus on coping and what helped, reviewed by the safety reviewer |
| 18 | Youth-facing content is reviewed by the safety reviewer | High, youth | Must fix if skipped | Named reviewer and date |

## How to write a finding

- **Location**: headline, sentence number, image, caption, comment reply template.
- **Issue**: the category and a paraphrase. Example: "Sentence 1 names the means and the
  place of death" (never repeat them). "Sentence 1 attributes the death to one event."
- **Severity**: Must fix, Should fix, or Consider.
- **Rewrite**: the full replacement sentence.

## Common rewrites (patterns, adapt to the organization's voice)

| Problem type | Pattern for the rewrite |
| --- | --- |
| Crime verb | "[Name] died by suicide in [season or year]." |
| Method or location | Remove entirely; do not replace with a euphemism for the method |
| Single cause | "Suicide is complex. Many things can build up, and support can help." |
| Suicide as inevitable | "Many people who have thoughts of suicide go on to find support and feel better." |
| Guilt ask | "Gifts in [Name]'s memory support [program description] for students." |
| Epidemic framing | State the figure with its source and pair it with a help-seeking action |
