# Agreement terms, activation, reporting and renewal

## Agreement terms checklist

Counsel drafts or reviews the agreement. Bring counsel a proposed position on each term:

| Term | What to decide |
|---|---|
| Parties and signatories | Legal names; who has authority to sign on each side |
| Term | Start and end dates; renewal mechanism |
| Payment | Amount, schedule, invoicing, what happens if a payment is missed |
| Purpose and restriction | Unrestricted, program-restricted or event-specific; how funds may be used |
| Benefits | The full benefit list from the ledger, with capacity limits; nothing implied beyond the list |
| Classification | Which benefits are acknowledgment and which are tangible, as confirmed by the accountant |
| Exclusivity | Category sponsor exclusivity if any; no exclusive-provider promises without accountant and counsel review |
| Name and logo license | Scope, approvals, turnaround, revocation |
| Cause marketing terms | Calculation per unit, cap, minimum guarantee, dates, accounting, payment timing, which party handles state requirements |
| Employee engagement | Volunteer roles, supervision, screening where volunteers may interact with minors, waivers, insurance |
| Data | No donor, participant or student data shared; how employee match data is handled by the company's platform |
| Content and IP | Ownership of co-created content; approvals; no use of participant images or stories |
| Publicity | Joint announcements approved by both sides |
| Reporting | What the organization will report, when, in what form |
| Reputation and termination | Either party may end the agreement for conduct that damages the other's reputation; notice and refund terms |
| Indemnification and insurance | Counsel sets |
| Confidentiality | What stays private |
| Governing law and disputes | Counsel sets |

## Activation plan

Activation is everything that turns a signed agreement into delivered value. Build a calendar with dates, owners, approvals and measures:

- kickoff meeting to confirm contacts, approvals path and reporting format;
- recognition deliverables (logo placements, announcements) with approval dates;
- employee engagement events: purpose, number of volunteers the program can host, supervision, screening, orientation;
- employee resource group campaigns: company approval, campaign window, matching instructions, tracking code;
- cause marketing launch: final consumer copy approved by counsel, reporting cadence for sales and donations;
- mid-term check-in on progress against commitments;
- end-of-term report and renewal conversation.

Keep a single log of every approval given for use of the organization's name and logo.

## Partner reporting

Report in the partner's terms: start from the priorities the company stated and the measures agreed.

| Include | Rules |
|---|---|
| What was delivered | Benefits provided against the ledger |
| Program activity supported | Counts of sessions, participants, materials; measured outcomes described as what was measured (participation, knowledge, self-reported confidence, help-seeking intentions), never as treatment effects |
| Employee engagement | Volunteers, hours, campaign participation, matched amounts |
| Stories | Only with recorded consent; de-identified unless the person chose otherwise; parental consent and organizational review for minors |
| Money | How funds were spent against any restriction; finance confirms figures |
| Next steps | What the next period could achieve |

Privacy: report aggregates, never participant-level data. Suppress any group smaller than the threshold the organization sets (state the threshold; if none is set, use a clearly labelled assumed default, for example fewer than 10, and ask the organization to confirm). Flag that student or health information may be covered by FERPA or HIPAA and that the organization's counsel decides applicability.

## Renewal

- Start the renewal conversation well before the term ends; the organization sets the lead time from the partner's budget cycle (ask when the company sets next year's budgets).
- Prepare a results review against the agreement's commitments and the partner's stated priorities, including anything that fell short and why.
- Ask the partner what they valued and what they would change.
- Propose the next term: continue, grow, or reshape; update the benefit ledger and flags.
- Record the partnership's internal champions and plan for their departure; a partnership held by one person at the company is fragile.
- If the partnership should end (poor fit, reputational concern, cost exceeds value), plan a respectful close and a final report.
