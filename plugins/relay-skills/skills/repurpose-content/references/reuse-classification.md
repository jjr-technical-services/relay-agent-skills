# Reuse classification

The point of classification is to spend review effort where meaning can change and
nowhere else. Classify each change, then give the asset the highest class of any change
it contains.

## The three classes

| Reuse class | Definition | Review | Typical turnaround |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Format only | Same approved words and meaning, new shape or medium | None; log the asset and its claim IDs | Same day |
| Wording change | Words differ from the source but every claim and its limits are intact | Light review by the content owner, who checks meaning against the register | Short |
| New claim or audience | Anything the source did not approve: new or altered claim, new audience, new context | Full review by the same roles that approved the source | As for new content |

## Decision rules, in order

Ask these in order and stop at the first yes.

1. Does the asset contain any statement that has no claim ID, or that states more than
   its claim ID (wider population, causal language, outcome language, dropped hedge,
   new comparison, outside data)? **New claim or audience.**
2. Is the audience outside the approved audiences for the source, or is the asset
   published in someone else's voice or under a co-brand? **New claim or audience.**
3. Does the asset move content into a more sensitive context: fundraising ask attached
   to findings, youth-facing channel, a crisis or suicide context, paid promotion, press?
   **New claim or audience.**
4. Are any words different from the approved wording, including headlines, simplified
   sentences, translations, merged sentences, reordered clauses, or approved rounding?
   **Wording change.**
5. Otherwise: **Format only.**

## Format only: what qualifies

- Verbatim excerpt of an approved sentence or paragraph, with its base and caveat intact.
- Splitting approved text across carousel slides, a thread, or email sections.
- Reformatting prose into bullets without changing words.
- Adding captions or a transcript that match the recorded speech.
- Alt text that describes the image and repeats only approved claims.
- Adding the organization-approved call to action and link.
- Resizing, re-cropping, or re-laying out approved graphics without changing numbers.

Cutting is Format only when what remains does not change meaning. Cutting a hedge,
base, or caveat is not a cut; it is a claim change.

## Wording change: what qualifies

- Paraphrase and plain-language rewrites for a lower reading level.
- New headlines, subject lines, hooks, and pull quotes that paraphrase.
- Approved rounding of a statistic (see claim-fidelity.md rule 1).
- Translation. The reviewer must read the target language; name that in the Reviewer
  column.
- Combining two approved sentences into one, when both claims stay separate in meaning.
- Tone shifts for a channel (more conversational, first person plural).

## New claim or audience: triggers to list

Name the exact trigger in the Review trigger column. Common triggers:

- Claim not in the register, or outside data added.
- Population widened, period dropped, or subgroup figure introduced.
- Correlation stated as cause; intention stated as behaviour; outcome or clinical
  language added.
- Audience not in the approved list: for example families when the source was approved
  for campus partners, youth when approved for adults, a corporate partner's employees,
  press, funders.
- Partner kit or co-branded asset: someone else will publish in their own voice.
- Findings used to support a donation ask or paid ad for the first time.
- Content touching suicide, self-harm, or crisis placed in a new context.
- A participant quote reused beyond the use the participant approved.

## Logging

Keep a reuse log row per asset: asset, date, claim IDs used, class, trigger, reviewer,
approval date. The log lets the next person reuse an already-reviewed Wording change
asset as a Format only source, provided they change nothing further.

## Worked example (illustrative, hypothetical)

Source approved for campus partners. Asset: an email section for families.

| Change from source | Class |
| --- | --- |
| Excerpted finding C2 verbatim | Format only |
| Simplified C4 to grade 7 | Wording change |
| Audience is families, not in approved list | New claim or audience |

Asset class: New claim or audience. Review trigger: "Audience not in approved list
(families)". Reviewer: communications lead plus program lead.
