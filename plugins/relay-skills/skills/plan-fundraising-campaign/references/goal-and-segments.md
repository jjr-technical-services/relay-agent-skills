# Goal build-up and segments

## Why bottom-up

A goal chosen as "what we need" or "last year plus 10%" hides the assumption that more people will give, or give more, without saying why. A bottom-up goal makes each assumption visible and testable after the campaign.

## Core formulas

For each segment s:

- `expected donors(s) = audience(s) × response rate(s)`
- `expected revenue(s) = expected donors(s) × average gift(s)`

Campaign:

- `segment revenue = Σ expected revenue(s)`
- `match earned = min(match cap, match ratio × revenue from match-eligible gifts)`
- `goal = segment revenue + leadership gifts committed in writing + match earned`

Peer-to-peer:

- `P2P revenue = fundraisers recruited × activation rate × average raised per active fundraiser`
- activation rate = fundraisers who raised at least one gift ÷ fundraisers who registered

Employee giving:

- `employee giving revenue = eligible employees reached × participation rate × average employee gift`
- `company match = min(company cap per employee or in total, match ratio × eligible employee gifts)`, per the company's written policy.

## Where the inputs come from

| Input | Source | If missing |
|---|---|---|
| Audience | Current CRM counts after exclusions | Cannot plan; get the count |
| Response rate | Same segment in the most comparable past campaign: donors ÷ people asked | Use the organization's closest other campaign and label Assumed; widen the low-high range |
| Average gift | Same segment, same campaign: total ÷ number of gifts | As above |
| Leadership gifts | Written commitments only | Treat verbal intentions as a separate "prospects" line, not in the goal |
| Match | Signed or written match terms | Leave out of the goal until confirmed |

Set low and high by using the lowest and highest of the organization's past values for that segment when there are several years, or a stated band around a single year's value (for example plus or minus a quarter of the rate), labelled Assumed.

## Closing a gap honestly

If leadership wants a higher number than the build-up supports, show the levers with arithmetic:

| Lever | Arithmetic | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| More leadership gifts before launch | named prospects × expected gift × likelihood | A prospect list with owners and ask dates |
| Larger reachable audience | new audience × response rate from a comparable segment | Consent and a real list, not a purchased list of strangers |
| A new or larger match | additional cap, only if eligible gifts can earn it | A committed matcher |
| Higher average gift | tested ask ladder change | A past test showing the change |
| Higher response rate | only with evidence of a change in approach | Past test or comparable campaign |

State plainly when the requested goal is not supported, and offer a stretch target separate from the plan goal.

## Segments

Useful segments for most campaigns (adjust to the organization's data):

| Segment | Inclusion rule | Typical treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Leadership prospects | Capacity and relationship for a gift above the leadership threshold | Personal ask before public launch |
| Last campaign donors | Gave to the same campaign last time | Thank for last gift, report what it did, ask to renew at or slightly above last gift |
| Other active donors | Gave in the last 12 months but not to this campaign | Invitation to join |
| Recurring donors | Active recurring gift | Thank; offer an optional extra gift only if the organization chooses to; never auto-upgrade |
| Lapsed donors | Last gift more than 12 months ago | Specific report on what their past giving supported, then a simple ask |
| Never-given contacts | Subscribers, event attendees, volunteers who have not given | First-gift invitation with a low-friction amount |
| Peer-to-peer fundraisers | Past fundraisers and volunteers | Recruitment and coaching |

Exclusions: people who opted out, donors in active major gift cultivation whose officer asks to hold them, deceased records, and families of someone being memorialized unless they asked to be included.

## Ask amounts

Base ask amounts on each donor's own history: last gift, or highest previous gift, with a modest ladder above it. For never-given contacts, use the organization's own median first gift. Never use program participation or service records to choose who is asked or how much.
