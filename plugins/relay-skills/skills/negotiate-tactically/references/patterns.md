# Negotiation Patterns

Use these as original, adaptable patterns. Do not present them as quotes from any book.

## Tone Ladder

### Soft

"I may be missing something, but it sounds like [constraint] is driving the decision. What would need to change for [desired outcome] to be workable?"

### Neutral

"It sounds like [constraint] is the issue. How should we solve for that while still getting to [desired outcome]?"

### Firm

"I understand [constraint]. I cannot commit to [unacceptable term]. What options do we have that keep [non-negotiable] intact?"

## Email Skeleton

```text
Subject: [Topic]

Hi [Name],

It sounds like [their concern or constraint] is the key issue. I understand why that matters.

From my side, [your constraint or boundary]. What would need to be true for us to move forward on [specific outcome]?

If useful, I can [reasonable next step].

Best,
[Name]
```

## Call Opening

```text
Before we get into solutions, I want to make sure I understand what is most important from your side. It sounds like [label]. What am I missing?
```

## Price Objection

```text
It sounds like the price is creating hesitation. What part of the proposal feels hardest to justify?
```

Follow-ups:

- "How are you measuring whether this is worth it?"
- "What would make the value clearer?"
- "If we changed scope, what would need to stay protected?"

## Deadline Pressure

```text
It sounds like timing is tight. I do not want to make a rushed commitment and miss something important. What is driving the deadline?
```

## Counteroffer Response

```text
I appreciate the counter. It sounds like [their constraint] is the main driver. I can work with [acceptable part], but [boundary]. How can we structure this so [shared goal] still happens?
```

## Saying No Without Burning Trust

```text
I understand why you are asking. I cannot agree to [request] because [short reason]. What I can do is [alternative]. Would that still help with [their underlying goal]?
```

## Closing Confirmation

```text
Here is what I have us aligned on:

- [Term 1]
- [Term 2]
- [Owner and date]
- [Open issue]

What did I miss?
```

## Debrief Prompts

- What did they repeat or emphasize?
- What did they avoid answering?
- Where did the tone change?
- What new constraint appeared?
- Did the user concede before diagnosing?
- What is the smallest next commitment to request?
