# Budget justification and reconciliation to the books

## What a reviewer looks for in each line

A budget justification (also called a budget narrative) answers, for every line: what is it, how was the amount calculated, and why does the program need it. A reviewer should be able to recompute every total from the justification alone.

Pattern for one line:

`Line name (amount). Calculation: unit cost × quantity × duration. Need: the activity or objective it supports. Basis: where the unit cost comes from (quote, policy, payroll, prior actuals).`

Rules:

- Use the funder's category names and order exactly, and the funder's template if one exists.
- Tie each line to an activity in the proposal and, where one exists, to the logic model (build-logic-model). A cost with no activity will be questioned.
- State the source of every rate: payroll records, the organization's fringe calculation, a vendor quote with date, the travel policy, a negotiated rate agreement.
- Keep the language factual. "Essential" and "critical" add nothing; the calculation and the link to an activity do the persuading.
- Respect the funder's page or character limit for the justification. When space is short, keep the calculation and the activity link, and drop adjectives.

## Illustrative justification rows (hypothetical round numbers)

| Line | Amount | Justification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Program coordinator | 30,000 | 60,000 annual salary × 50% effort × 12 months. Leads workshop scheduling, facilitator coordination, and attendance tracking for objective 1. Salary from the current payroll record. |
| Fringe | 6,000 | 20% of salary charged (30,000 × 0.20), the organization's fringe rate for the current fiscal year, covering employer payroll taxes, health, and retirement contributions. |
| Workshop materials | 4,000 | 40 workshops × 100 per workshop for printed guides and supplies, based on last year's actual cost per workshop. |
| Youth advisory stipends | 6,000 | 12 members × 500 per year, paid under the organization's participant stipend policy. Participant support cost; excluded from the MTDC base. |
| Program space | 12,000 | Annual rent of 48,000 × 500 of 2,000 square feet used by the program. Allocation basis: floor plan dated at budget preparation. Rental cost; excluded from the MTDC base. |

Check: 60,000 × 0.50 = 30,000; 30,000 × 0.20 = 6,000; 40 × 100 = 4,000; 12 × 500 = 6,000; 48,000 × 500 ÷ 2,000 = 12,000.

## Multi-year budgets

- One column per budget year plus a total. Each year must stand alone if the funder awards year by year.
- Show escalation (salary increases, vendor price changes) as a labelled assumption with its effective month.
- Explain any year-to-year swing larger than the escalation in one sentence tied to the program timeline (for example, a pilot year followed by expansion to a second site).
- For multi-year federal awards, note that later years are usually subject to continuation approval and availability of funds.

## Reconciliation to the chart of accounts

Before submission, map every budget line to the organization's general ledger so the award can be tracked when it arrives.

| Budget line | Funder category | GL account | Grant or class code | Source system |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Program coordinator | Personnel | Salaries and wages | Grant code for this award | Payroll allocation by grant |
| Fringe | Fringe | Payroll taxes; benefits | Same grant code | Payroll allocation by grant |
| Workshop materials | Supplies | Program supplies | Same grant code | Card receipts and grant coding |

Checks:

- Each line maps to at least one account; any unmapped line is flagged.
- Personnel lines match the effort that payroll allocation by grant will apply. If payroll allocates by fixed percentages, the percentages must equal the budgeted effort, and they must be updated from time records when actual effort differs.
- The grant code set up in the accounting system (new grant setup) carries the award period, so costs outside the period are rejected.
- Categories that the funder treats differently from the chart of accounts (for example, the funder's "contractual" spans two GL accounts) are listed so monthly reports can roll up correctly.

## Common reviewer objections and how the budget prevents them

| Objection | Prevention |
| --- | --- |
| "Effort is not credible" | Effort matches the activities and the staffing plan in the narrative |
| "Indirect exceeds our policy" | Indirect cost check against the funder's wording, with the formula shown |
| "Budget does not match the narrative" | Every activity in the narrative has a cost line and every line has an activity |
| "Supplanting" (grant replaces existing funding) | Narrative states what the grant adds beyond current funded activity |
| "Unsupported match" | In-kind match lists the donor, item, and valuation basis |
