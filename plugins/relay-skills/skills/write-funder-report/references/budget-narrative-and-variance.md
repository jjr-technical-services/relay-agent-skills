# Budget-to-actual narrative for a funder report

## Build the comparison

Use the grant's approved budget (including approved modifications) and actual expenses coded to the grant through the report period end, from the accounting system or the monthly grant spending report.

| Line | Budget for period | Actual | Variance | Variance % | Flag | Explanation | Approval needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Formulas:

`variance = actual − budget`

`variance % = variance ÷ budget × 100`

Decide with the user whether "budget for period" is the full award or the phased budget to date. For an interim report, a phased comparison is usually more meaningful; state which one is shown.

## Applying the funder's threshold

Quote the agreement's rule and apply it exactly. Rules vary:

- a percentage of each line or category;
- a percentage of the total award, sometimes cumulative across transfers;
- a fixed amount;
- any change at all to certain lines (for example personnel or equipment) regardless of size.

Mark each line "Within threshold" or "Beyond threshold". If the rule is ambiguous, apply the stricter reading and ask the program officer.

Illustrative (hypothetical round numbers), with a 10% per-line rule:

| Line | Budget | Actual | Variance | Variance % | Flag |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Personnel | 40,000 | 31,000 | −9,000 | −22.5% | Beyond threshold |
| Travel | 5,000 | 5,400 | +400 | +8.0% | Within threshold |
| Supplies | 8,000 | 6,000 | −2,000 | −25.0% | Beyond threshold |
| Total | 53,000 | 42,400 | −10,600 | −20.0% | |

Check: 40,000 + 5,000 + 8,000 = 53,000; 31,000 + 5,400 + 6,000 = 42,400; 42,400 − 53,000 = −10,600; −10,600 ÷ 53,000 = −20.0%.

## Explaining variances

For each line beyond threshold: cause (timing, volume, rate, scope), effect on deliverables, and action (re-phase, request a modification, return funds, or no action). Keep it factual and connected to the program narrative; the personnel underspend should match the staffing delay described in the outcomes section.

Underspending is not automatically good news for a funder: it can mean the program reached fewer people. Say what the unspent money means for the work and what the organization proposes.

## When a modification or approval is needed

If the variance or a planned change crosses the agreement's approval rule, the report is not the place to ask for forgiveness. Flag "Approval needed: yes", state whether a request has been sent, and hand the request to build-grant-budget. Costs incurred on an unapproved plan risk disallowance.

## Consistency checks

- Totals match the accounting system for the period.
- Cost share or match reported matches its documentation.
- Indirect costs charged stay within the approved rate and cap.
- The narrative explanation and the numbers tell the same story.
